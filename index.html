Method & caveats

  • Listings: Redfin public search results and listing pages (houses with 3+ beds, 1.5+ baths, 1+ garage) for Chittenden, Franklin, Lamoille, Washington, Grand Isle and Addison counties. Photos and links go to Redfin.
  • Drive times: OSRM public router from Richmond and Milton villages. OSRM can run slightly fast versus real traffic, so homes at 42–48 min are flagged borderline. Homes at 45–48 min are on the Near misses tab (their condition wasn't checked).
  • Elevation: USGS 3DEP (EPQS) at each listing's map coordinates, kept if above 500 ft.
  • Condition: listing remarks were read; fixer-uppers, as-is, projects, unbuilt and multi-family homes were removed. Rows with vague wording, no remarks, or public-record mismatches (e.g. manufactured) are flagged rather than dropped.
  • Budget badges: In budget ≤ $600K · Stretch $600–650K · Over > $650K. The default view caps price at $650K; clear Max price to see everything.
  • Gaps: Redfin returned 740 of 759 Chittenden County sales, so about 19 sold homes from the past year are missing. Data can lag Redfin by up to a day.
